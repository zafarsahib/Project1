About Us

Helping pets find loving and responsible homes.

Our Pet Adoption System

The Pet Adoption Management System is designed to make the pet adoption process simple, organized, and accessible.

Visitors can browse available pets, create an account, and submit adoption requests. Administrators can manage pets and review adoption applications.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a clear and user-friendly platform that connects people with pets that are looking for new homes.

The system also helps administrators keep track of pets and adoption requests throughout the adoption process.

How It Works

1. Browse Pets

Explore the pets available for adoption and view their details.

2. Submit a Request

Registered users can submit an adoption request for a pet they are interested in.

3. Find a New Companion

Administrators review requests and manage the adoption process.
